Repairing C vulnerabilities with small open LLMs

SUTD · Team of 5 · Apr 2026

Given only a vulnerable C function, generate a patch with a 7B model, then verify it by compiling with AddressSanitizer and re-running the exploit.

  • ProblemCan small open 7B models fix real C vulnerabilities given only the function, with no crash logs and no known bug location?
  • ApproachThree ML signals feed a few-shot prompt; each patch must compile under AddressSanitizer and stop the exploit.
  • Result15.4% of 52 real CVEs fixed (Pass@5, llama3:8b). Adding retrieval lowered compile rates for every model.
  • My roleBuilt the retrieval module (CodeT5+ and FAISS, under 5 ms per query) and the full evaluation harness, and designed the ablations.

How far does English GPT-2 transfer? Multilingual NLI

SUTD Natural Language Processing · Group project · Nov – Dec 2025

Our group implemented GPT-2 and fine-tuned it for natural language inference in English. I tested how well that transfers to 14 other languages, and what predicts the result.

  • ProblemHow well does an English-trained GPT-2 handle 14 other languages, and what predicts how well it does?
  • ApproachZero-shot tests on 15 XNLI languages, tokenizer fertility analysis, then per-language vs unified fine-tuning.
  • ResultFertility predicts zero-shot accuracy (Spearman −0.818). Fine-tuning added 18–25 points.
  • My roleRan the multilingual study and all of the extended analysis. Implementing GPT-2 was group work.
