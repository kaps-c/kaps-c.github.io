Understanding GPT-2 across 15 languages

A multilingual evaluation and interpretability study examining how tokenisation affects cross-lingual transfer.

  • My roleLed the evaluation and designed experiments across zero-shot, per-language and multilingual fine-tuning settings.
  • FindingDeveloped tokenizer fertility metrics and identified token fragmentation as a major bottleneck in multilingual generalisation.
  • EvidenceA linear probing classifier achieved 94% tone-classification accuracy within the study.
Inside the evaluation

The study combined performance evaluation with attention entropy, head dispersion, t-SNE representation visualisation and a multilingual error taxonomy.

The 94% result refers specifically to the study’s tone-classification probe, rather than overall multilingual language-model performance.
